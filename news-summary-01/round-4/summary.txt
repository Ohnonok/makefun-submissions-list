11 November 2026: diplomacy, climate and a record AI listing led the day — Abu Dhabi talks, Antalya's COP31, Anthropic's $1.4tn IPO, and a market rebound.
Russia and Ukraine ended US-mediated Abu Dhabi talks with the war's largest prisoner exchange but no ceasefire or territory deal (Reuters).
Anthropic's IPO roadshow priced 825m shares at $70–$78, valuing it at up to $1.4tn, with a $26bn private placement; Bloomberg: orders several times oversubscribed.
CoreWeave revenue jumped 168% to $3.66bn, lifting AI shares; the S&P 500 rose 1.3% and the Nasdaq 1.6% despite October inflation (+0.4%) (Reuters).
COP31 opened in Antalya, where UN chief Guterres warned the slow climate response has put the world 'on a path to destruction' (AFP).
The Strait of Hormuz stayed shut a 250th day, Iran demanding the US answer 7 conditions, after 3 tankers caught fire on naval mines (Al Jazeera).
Israeli strikes killed at least 7 in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son, health officials said; the military said it hit militants (Reuters).
Spain held its first general strike in 14 years, with 4m workers out over housing costs (CNN).
The US House voted 211–204 to send the Hegseth impeachment resolution to committee, ending the effort for this Congress (NPR).
Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to Ebola-free, while the DRC outbreak hit 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths (Reuters).
Trump and Xi are expected to meet at the APEC summit in Shenzhen on 18–19 November (Reuters).
Russia's US ambassador, Alexander Darchiev, died suddenly in Moscow aged 66, the Foreign Ministry said, on the same day as the Abu Dhabi talks (Reuters).
Sudan's army said it downed 2 FH-95 drones in West Kordofan and East Darfur, its 29th claimed downing, as drone strikes on power stations cut power and water (Anadolu Agency).
Yemen's government said it killed or wounded about 190 Houthis across 6 provinces; the WHO counts at least 1,771 killed since August (Al Jazeera).
